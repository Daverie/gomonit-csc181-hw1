## 1. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  
I am designng for visitors who wants to go around Iligan via jeepney.

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  
Mobile data services might be temporarily disabled during certain parts of the festival, therefore, it is important to have an offline map of the jeepney routes.

---

## 2. Situation or need

**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**  
The visitors are trying to go around Iligan via jeepney to visit different events and attractions.

---

## 3. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**  
Locating jeepney routes and stops can be confusing for visitors who are not familiar with the city. 

---

## 4. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?**  
A mobile dashboard that shows you the jeepney route of your choice, the route shall include landmarks for tourist reference and use trending data to know how the locals navigate these places. This dashboard will be available offline to ensure accessibility even with limited mobile data.

**How would it help the intended users?**  
This application will help them navigate the city easily without the need for mobile data. It will also help them discover new places and events that they may not have known about otherwise.

---

## 5. Things users can do

Describe **two specific actions** that users could perform using your proposed system.

1. Choose their destination and the app will show them the a jeepney route of their choice.
2. The user may choose a featured landmark and click it to know what jeepneys can take you there.

---

## 6. Important qualities

It is a portable and reliable navigation system that is available offline.

### Quality 1: Reliable

**Why does this matter to users?**  
This quality is important because the users are tourists who are not familiar with the city. They need to be able to rely on the app to provide them with accurate information about the jeepney routes and the app is available offline to ensure accessibility even with limited mobile data.

### Quality 2: Portable

**Why does this matter to users?**  
This quality is important because the users are tourists who are on the go. They need to be able to use the app on their mobile phones without the need for a computer or to be tethered to a specific location.

---

## 7. How to tell whether the solution helped

How could you determine whether your proposed solution actually helped users?

We can use a survey to ask users if they were able to find the information they needed and if they were able to use the app to navigate the city. We can also observe whether users can complete a task more easily and check whether fewer people miss event updates.
